What Mix Doctor checks

Balance

Bass, low-mid, mid, high-mid and air against your reference.

Spectral congestion

Crowded ranges and likely masking, from the low end to the upper mids.

Dynamics

Crest factor and transient punch — over-compressed or too loose.

Stereo image

Width against the reference, phase and mono compatibility.

Loudness & peaks

BS.1770 integrated loudness and 4× oversampled true peak.

Reference differences

Where your mix and your reference part ways, in dB.

Tools to act on it

When you know what to fix, the tools are already on the track.

Reference Match

Linear-phase match EQ that reshapes your mix toward a reference — level-neutral, click-free, linked or mid/side, with the correction curve shown before you apply it

8-Band EQ

Analog-matched bells, shelves, cuts and notch, placed on stereo, mid or side, edited right on the analyzer

BS.1770 Metering

Gated integrated LUFS, 4× oversampled true peak, crest factor, width and phase — your mix and the reference side by side

Trace Mode

Draw a target curve on the analyzer (or over a screenshot) and match to it instead of a reference

Spectrum Analyzer

1/12-octave analyzer with peak hold, focus groups for bass, vocal, drum and synth ranges, reference overlay

Session Recall

Reference analysis, trace and settings are saved with your session — even if the reference file moves
