MixMind

VST3 & AU · macOS 12+

Reference-matching telemetry shaper. Load a reference track and MixMind overlays its spectrum against your live audio, then designs a linear-phase match EQ to close the gap. It measures — you decide.

Beta build shared directly with testers while the current package is rebuilt

Reference Overlay

Load a reference track (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Ogg, MP3). Its spectrum is resampled to your session rate and drawn pastel against your saturated live curve.

Focus Groups

Bass, guitar, vocals, drums — each gets its own hue, so you compare one group at a time instead of reading the whole spectrum.

Match Shaping

Auto mode targets reference minus live; manual mode targets your drawn trace minus live. 1/3-octave smoothed, linear-phase FIR, ±24 dB clamp.

Honest Metering

BS.1770-4 integrated LUFS, 4× true peak, crest factor, stereo width and phase correlation — your mix measured against the reference.

Master Spectrum

Log-scale analyzer with peak hold, 0 dB reference, and the cow-print master curve for band-level reads at a glance.

Session Recall

The match curve and reference are saved with the session, and the shaper keeps running with the editor closed.
