MixMind
by JuicePipe

AI-Powered Mixing Advice, Inside Your DAW

MixMind listens to your mix in real time and gives you context-aware advice — no tab switching, no describing what you hear.

Download for macOS

Version 1.0.0 · VST3 & AU · macOS 12+

What It Does

🎛️ Real-time FFT spectrum analysis on your master bus
📊 LUFS loudness, true peak, stereo width tracking
💬 Ask anything — "Is my low end muddy?" "What's eating the vocal?"
🧠 DeepSeek-powered answers based on your actual mix data
⏱️ BPM & time signature sync from your DAW transport
🔒 No audio leaves your machine — only analysis data is sent

How to Install

  1. Download and run the .pkg installer
  2. Open Ableton Live, Logic Pro, or any VST3/AU host
  3. Load MixMind on your master bus
  4. Enter your beta license key in the plugin settings
  5. Press play and start asking questions
